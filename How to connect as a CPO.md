# Connect as a CPO

ich-tanke-strom.ch collects charging infrastructure data from Charge Point Operators (CPOs) across Switzerland and makes it publicly available. If you operate charging points and want to connect to the data infrastructure, you deliver your data to ich-tanke-strom.ch.

ich-tanke-strom.ch requires data that complies with the **Open Charge Point Interface (OCPI)**. OCPI is a standardised, open communication protocol for electric mobility that enables data exchange between the different players in the charging infrastructure sector.

## Guide for CPOs

The guide describes how to connect and deliver your data. It is available in four languages:

* [English](https://pubdb.bfe.admin.ch/en/publication/download/12784)
* [Deutsch](https://pubdb.bfe.admin.ch/de/publication/download/12784)
* [Français](https://pubdb.bfe.admin.ch/fr/publication/download/12784)
* [Italiano](https://pubdb.bfe.admin.ch/it/publication/download/12784)
